      Progressive learning path

      Stage lessons

      Stage 1–3 with examples

      Stage 1

      Foundations

      Rules, hand ranks, betting rounds

      Quickly spot the strongest hand and what actions are allowed each street.

      • Hand ladder: Royal > Straight Flush > Four of a Kind > Full House > Flush > Straight > Trips > Two Pair > One Pair > High Card.
      • Action order: Preflop blinds post first; action clockwise. Flop/Turn/River each has bet/check/raise/fold.
      • Example: You hold A♠ K♠ on J♠ 8♠ 2♦ — strong flush draw; plan to continue vs bets.
      Stage 2

      Core math

      Pot odds, outs, EV as price vs. value

      Decide fast: compare your price to your chance to win.

      • Outs: Flush draw = 9 outs; open-ender = 8 outs; two overcards ≈ 6 outs.
      • Pot odds bubble: Call ÷ (Pot + Call). If 1.2 to win 4.1 → ~29% needed. Flush draw on flop ≈ 35% → call is good.
      • Example: Pot 3 BB, villain bets 1 BB. Need 1 / (3+1+1) = 20%. Any 8-out straight draw ≈ 31% → call.
      Stage 3

      Position & ranges

      Info advantage and colored ranges

      Acting last lets you widen opens and apply pressure.

      • Late position (Button/CO): open wider; add suited connectors and suited aces.
      • Early position: play tight—pairs, big aces, strong broadways.
      • Example range overlay: BTN opens (green) all pairs, all suited aces, K9s+, Q9s+, J9s+, T8s+, 98s-54s, ATo+, KJo+.
      • Blind defense: call suited connectors/pairs vs small opens; 3-bet offsuit broadways vs frequent stealers.
